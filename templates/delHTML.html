School Days
  • 15 School Days - SST due after receipt of parent request for evaluation. Offer Consent or PWN by this date.
  • 45 School Days - Special Request Evaluation due.
Calendar Days
  • 20 Calendar Days - District policy due date for Initial ARDs.
  • 30 Calendar Days - Legal due date for Initial ARDS. (ALWAYS use Plan Management to calculate this date!)
  • 30 Calendar Days - District policy due date for ARD to review Special Request Eval.

The date of the triggering event (e.g., receipt of parent request for evaluation, receipt of consent for evaluation, etc.) is Day 0. The day following the triggering even is always Day 1 in the count.
